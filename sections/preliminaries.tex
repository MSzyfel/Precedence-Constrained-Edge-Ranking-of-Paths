\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:preliminaries}

Given a path graph $P$ and a DAG $T$ on the edges of $P$ an precedence constrained edge ranking of $P$ with respect to $T$ is a function $\ell\colon E(P_n) \to \mathbb{N}$ such that for every two edges $e_1,e_2$ with $\ell(e_1)=\ell(e_2)$, there is an edge $e$ on the path between $e_1$ and $e_2$ such that $\ell(e)<\ell(e_1)$, and for every two edges $e_1,e_2$ such that there is a directed path from $e_1$ to $e_2$ in $T$, we have $\ell(e_1)<\ell(e_2)$. By $\precrankmax(P,T)$ we denote the \emph{precedence constrained ranking number} of $\br{P,T}$, i. e. the minimum number of labels needed for a precedence constrained edge ranking of $P$ with respect to $T$. By $\precranksum(P,T)$ we denote the \emph{min-sum precedence constrained ranking number} of $\br{P,T}$, i. e. the minimum sum of the labels assigned to the edges of $P$ in a precedence constrained edge ranking of $P$ with respect to $T$. The \emph{precedence constrained edge ranking problem} is to compute $\precrankmax(P,T)$ and the \emph{min-sum precedence constrained edge ranking problem} is to compute $\precranksum(P,T)$.
