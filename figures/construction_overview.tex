\caption{Overview of the reduction from 3-SAT. Variable gadgets for $x_1, x_2, x_3, \ldots, x_n$ are shown on top, and three example clause gadgets $C_1 = \br{x_1 \vee \bar{x}_2 \vee x_3}$, $C_2 = \br{\bar{x}_1 \vee x_3 \vee x_n}$, and $C_3 = \br{x_2 \vee \bar{x}_3 \vee \bar{x}_n}$ are shown below. Dashed arrows between variable and clause gadgets indicate precedence constraints. Blue arrows correspond to positive literals (from $e_{x_i}$~or~$e_{x_i}'$), red arrows correspond to negated literals (from $e_{\bar{x}_i}$~or~$e_{\bar{x}_i}'$).}
